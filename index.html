    01 · Inspiration

    Wings of fire, a mind for infinity

    Two Indian scientists shaped how I think about work. One taught me to aim at the sky, the other that beauty and rigour belong together.

    Dr. Kalam rose from a small coastal town to lead India's rocket and missile programmes. He reminds me that ambition matters only when it serves people. Ramanujan, with almost no formal training, filled notebooks with results mathematicians are still proving. He reminds me that curiosity and discipline compound. My own work sits between the two: space-grade reliability and mathematical precision, built into real systems.

    When G. H. Hardy visited Ramanujan in hospital, he remarked that his taxi's number, 1729, seemed rather dull. Ramanujan replied that it was very interesting: it is the smallest number expressible as the sum of two cubes in two different ways.

    1729 = 1³ + 12³ = 9³ + 10³
